# Business & Finance Video Timestamp Location Prompt

## Core principles: precise, complete, clear business logic

1.  **Precise location**: timestamps must correspond strictly to the core discussion of the business topic.
2.  **Completeness**: the time range must fully cover all the key arguments of a business topic, so the business logic is complete.
3.  **Natural boundaries**: prefer to place time points where a business viewpoint changes, a case study switches, or a logical section ends.

## Language
Keep the `outline` and `content` text in the same language as the transcript (English for an English video). Do not translate.

---

## Key instructions: how to decide `start_time` and `end_time`

### Deciding `start_time`:
-   Place it at the start time of the **first core statement** about the business topic.
-   Ignore unrelated small talk, disclaimers or transition phrases before that statement.
-   **Prefer**: start at a boundary in the business logic so the topic is introduced naturally and clearly.
-   **Business content**: if there is important background or a data reference that sets it up, move the start earlier accordingly.

### Deciding `end_time` (most important):
-   It **must** be the **end timestamp of the last sentence** covering the core discussion of the business topic.
-   **Complete business logic**: make sure the business analysis, investment advice, risk warnings and other core content are complete, with no gaps in the logic.
-   **Natural ending**: prefer to end at a summarizing sentence, the conclusion of the investment advice, or a topic change.
-   **Include risk warnings**: if there is an important risk warning or disclaimer at the end of the topic, include it.
-   If the discussion of the business topic continues to the end of the provided SRT chunk, `end_time` should be the end timestamp of the last relevant subtitle.
-   **Wrong approach**: blindly setting `end_time` to the end of the whole chunk. This must be avoided at all costs.

### Duration control for business content:
-   **Target length**: each business topic segment should be between 3 and 8 minutes
-   **Complex analysis**: complex, multi-dimensional business topics can be extended to 10 minutes
-   **Quick news**: market news flashes and policy briefs can be compressed to 2–3 minutes
-   **Deep analysis**: in-depth content such as industry research and company analysis can be extended to 12 minutes

### Special handling for business content:
-   **Data-heavy passages**: passages with a lot of financial or market data should be kept whole
-   **Case studies**: a complete business case study should stay together; avoid splitting it
-   **Investment advice**: investment strategies and risk warnings should appear together and must not be separated
-   **Policy analysis**: policy content and its impact analysis should stay complete

---

## Input format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
-   `outline`: a JSON array of **several** business topics to process.
-   `srt_text`: the **single** SRT chunk related to this batch of topics, in the format `index\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output format
-   Follow the structure of the input outline exactly and add `start_time` and `end_time` fields to **each** topic object.
-   **Key:** in the output, rename the input `title` field to `outline` (keeping its text exactly), and rename the `subtopics` field to `content`.
-   Output one JSON array containing **all** processed topics.
-   Make sure times use the format `HH:MM:SS,mmm`.

**Strict JSON output requirements:**
1. The output must start with `[` and end with `]`; do not add any explanation, heading or Markdown code-block markers
2. Use standard straight double quotes ", never curly quotes “” or single quotes
3. Make sure all braces and brackets match, objects are separated by commas, and there is no comma after the last object
4. Quotes inside strings must be escaped as \"
5. Do not include comments, extra text or control characters
6. Make sure the JSON is completely valid and can be parsed by a standard JSON parser

## Examples of locating business content

### Correct location:
- **Investment strategy discussion**: starts at "I think the current market..." and ends at "That's my investment advice"
- **Company analysis**: starts at "Let's look at this company..." and ends at "the overall assessment"
- **Industry trends**: starts at "The trend in this industry..." and ends at "the outlook"

### Mistakes to avoid:
- Cutting off in the middle of an explanation of financial data
- Separating investment advice from its risk warning
- Cutting at a key argument in a case study
- Leaving out important market context

## Notes

1. **Complete business logic**: make sure each segment contains a complete line of business analysis
2. **Data accuracy**: important financial and market data must be included in full
3. **Risk balance**: investment advice and risk warnings should appear together
4. **Timeliness**: keep market news tight; deep analysis can run longer
5. **Audience fit**: consider the needs of audiences with different investment experience
