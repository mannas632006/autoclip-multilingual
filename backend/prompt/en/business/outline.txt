# Business & Finance Video Outline Extraction Prompt

## Input format
You will receive a JSON input:

{
  "text": "{a segment of the video transcript}"
}

- The `text` field is one segment of the video transcript;
- The content has been checked and is broadly coherent;
- Use only the input as your source of information. Do not add outside knowledge, common-sense inferences or logical filler.

## Language
Write every topic name and subtopic in the same language as the transcript (English for an English video). Do not translate.

## Core task: business value and practicality together
Your task is to **fully cover** every important business topic in the input text while staying **reasonably concise** and avoiding over-fragmentation. The goal is to extract **8–15 core topics**, each corresponding to **3–8 minutes** of video.

## Topic extraction principles

### 1. Business value first
- **Practicality first**: prioritize the business views and analysis most valuable to investors, entrepreneurs and professionals
- **Data driven**: focus on content with specific data, case studies and market trends
- **No empty talk**: skip purely theoretical discussion and focus on actionable business strategies and practical experience

### 2. Timeliness
- **Hot topics**: prioritize content on current market hot spots, policy changes and industry developments
- **Trend analysis**: focus on predictions and analysis of where business is heading
- **Timing**: highlight time-sensitive content such as investment timing and windows of business opportunity

### 3. Logical structure
- **Cause and effect**: make sure each topic contains a complete chain of business logic (cause – process – result)
- **Risk and return**: balance discussion of business opportunities with potential risks
- **Multiple dimensions**: cover different levels such as the macro economy, industry analysis and company fundamentals

### 4. Audience fit
- **Layered value**: distinguish content suited to different audiences (beginner vs. advanced)
- **Practical guidance**: prioritize content with concrete advice and tool recommendations
- **Rich in cases**: focus on real business cases and shared experience of successes and failures

## Merging and splitting strategy

### Merge (these should become one topic):
- Different angles of analysis on the same company or industry
- Related investment strategies or business models
- Multi-dimensional interpretation of the same economic phenomenon
- Continuous market analysis and forecasting

### Split (these should become separate topics):
- A shift from the macro economy to analysis of a specific company
- Independent discussions of different industries or markets
- Investment strategy discussed separately from risk management
- A clear separation between theoretical analysis and practical advice

## Output format

Output strictly in the following format, with no other descriptive text:

### Outline:
1.  **[Top-level topic name]**
    - [Subtopic 1: highlight business value and practicality]
    - [Subtopic 2: highlight business value and practicality]
    - …

2.  **[Top-level topic name]**
    - [Subtopic 1]
    - …

Formatting rules (the output is parsed by a program):
- Each topic line starts with its number, a period, and the topic name wrapped in double asterisks: `1.  **Topic name**`
- Each subtopic is on its own line starting with `- `
- Do not use asterisks anywhere else in a topic line, and do not use numbered lines for subtopics

## Output example

### Outline:
1.  **Current market investment opportunities**
    - Judging the timing of a valuation recovery in tech stocks
    - Reading the policy tailwinds for the clean-energy sector
    - Key indicators to watch for a recovery in consumer stocks
    - How to position for capital flowing back into overseas markets

2.  **Startup valuation and fundraising strategy**
    - Valuation negotiation tactics for a pre-Series A round
    - The financial metrics investors focus on in due diligence
    - Practical experience balancing dilution and control
    - Pacing fundraising across different stages of growth

3.  **Industry trends and business model innovation**
    - How the profit model of live-stream shopping has evolved
    - Optimizing customer acquisition cost for SaaS companies
    - Supply-chain innovation in new retail formats
    - Compliance strategies for platform businesses under regulation

## Notes

1. **Business sensitivity**: make sure the extracted topics have clear business value and practicality
2. **Data accuracy**: focus on content with specific data, percentages and dates
3. **Risk disclosure**: balance opportunities and risks; avoid one-sided optimism or pessimism
4. **Timeliness**: prioritize time-sensitive and urgent business information
5. **Audience fit**: consider the understanding and needs of audiences with different business backgrounds
