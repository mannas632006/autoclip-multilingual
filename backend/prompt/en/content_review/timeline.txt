# Commentary & Review Video Timestamp Location Prompt

## Core principles: complete commentary, clear logic, accurate information

1.  **Complete commentary**: timestamps must correspond strictly to complete commentary units and passages.
2.  **Logical coherence**: the time range must fully cover the commentary's line of reasoning, so the analysis is complete.
3.  **Natural boundaries**: prefer to place time points at natural boundaries such as plot transitions, theme changes or the end of a commentary passage.

## Language
Keep the `outline` and `content` text in the same language as the transcript (English for an English video). Do not translate.

---

## Key instructions: how to decide `start_time` and `end_time`

### Deciding `start_time`:
-   Place it at the start time of the **first core sentence** of the commentary topic.
-   Ignore unrelated transitions, background music, intros or outros before it.
-   **Prefer**: start where the topic is clearly raised or the commentary begins, so the commentary is complete.
-   **Commentary content**: if there is important background, a clip from the work or a setup, move the start earlier accordingly.

### Deciding `end_time` (most important):
-   It **must** be the **end timestamp of the last sentence** covering the core commentary of the topic.
-   **Complete commentary**: make sure the plot commentary, analysis, evaluation and technique explanation are complete, with no gaps.
-   **Natural ending**: prefer to end at a summarizing sentence, a verdict or a topic change.
-   **Keep information**: if the end of the commentary includes important additional information, an easter egg or a clip from the work, include it.
-   If the discussion of the topic continues to the end of the provided SRT chunk, `end_time` should be the end timestamp of the last relevant subtitle.
-   **Wrong approach**: blindly setting `end_time` to the end of the whole chunk. This must be avoided at all costs.

### Duration control for commentary content:
-   **Target length**: each commentary topic segment should be between 3 and 8 minutes
-   **Complex commentary**: in-depth analysis or complex plots can be extended to 10 minutes
-   **Simple introductions**: basic background or simple concepts can be compressed to 2–3 minutes
-   **Important content**: core plot commentary or key analysis can be extended to 12 minutes

### Special handling for commentary content:
-   **Complete plot**: a plot commentary with a full setup, development, turn and resolution should stay together
-   **Analysis process**: important analysis and reasoning should be kept whole
-   **Clips from the work**: clips that accompany the commentary should stay in sync with it
-   **Technique explanations**: a technique demonstration and its explanation should appear together and must not be separated

---

## Input format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
-   `outline`: a JSON array of **several** commentary topics to process.
-   `srt_text`: the **single** SRT chunk related to this batch of topics, in the format `index\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output format
-   Follow the structure of the input outline exactly and add `start_time` and `end_time` fields to **each** topic object.
-   **Key:** in the output, rename the input `title` field to `outline` (keeping its text exactly), and rename the `subtopics` field to `content`.
-   Output one JSON array containing **all** processed topics.
-   Make sure times use the format `HH:MM:SS,mmm`.

**Strict JSON output requirements:**
1. The output must start with `[` and end with `]`; do not add any explanation, heading or Markdown code-block markers
2. Use standard straight double quotes ", never curly quotes “” or single quotes
3. Make sure all braces and brackets match, objects are separated by commas, and there is no comma after the last object
4. Quotes inside strings must be escaped as \"
5. Do not include comments, extra text or control characters
6. Make sure the JSON is completely valid and can be parsed by a standard JSON parser

## Examples of locating commentary content

### Correct location:
- **Plot commentary**: starts at "Next, let's look at this key scene..." and ends at "That's the core meaning of this part of the story"
- **Character analysis**: starts at "Let's break down this character..." and ends at "That's the deeper meaning of the character"
- **Technique explanation**: from the introduction of the technique to the end of the demonstration and the explanation of its effect
- **Background**: from the start of the background to the summary of how it connects to the theme

### Mistakes to avoid:
- Cutting off at a key point of analysis in the plot commentary
- Separating a technique explanation from its demonstration
- Cutting in the middle of an in-depth character reading
- Leaving out important additional information or clips from the work

## Special considerations by commentary type

### 1. Film and TV commentary:
- Keep plot commentary complete and logical
- Keep important character analysis and relationship readings
- Include the film clips that accompany the commentary
- Keep technique analysis professional and accurate

### 2. Game commentary:
- Keep gameplay and commentary in sync
- Include complete walkthrough steps and skill demonstrations
- Keep important game background and world introductions
- Convey the gaming experience faithfully

### 3. Analysis of creative works:
- Include the complete analysis and reasoning
- Keep theory and examples together
- Keep the cultural and historical context complete
- Include important judgements of value and meaning

### 4. Science explainers:
- Keep explanations systematic and logical
- Include complete experiments and explanations of principles
- Keep concept explanations accurate and accessible
- Keep important applications and extensions

## Timestamp strategies for different commentary content

### Plot-driven commentary:
- Locate by plot development: setup – development – climax and turn – resolution
- Keep the plot commentary complete and continuous
- Include important plot analysis and deeper meaning
- Make sure viewers can follow the whole plot

### Technique-driven commentary:
- Locate by type of technique: basic – intermediate – advanced application – live demonstration
- Keep technique explanations systematic and practical
- Include complete demonstrations and results
- Make sure the technique can be learned and repeated

### Analysis-driven commentary:
- Locate by level of analysis: surface phenomenon – underlying cause – impact and meaning – judgement of value
- Keep the analysis logical and deep
- Include important reasoning and conclusions
- Keep the analysis objective and persuasive

### Knowledge-driven commentary:
- Locate by knowledge structure: basic concepts – core principles – examples of application – extensions
- Keep knowledge delivery systematic and accurate
- Include important concept explanations and principles
- Make sure the knowledge is useful and understandable

## Notes

1. **Complete commentary**: make sure each segment contains a complete line of commentary and structure
2. **Accuracy**: important facts and technical details must be included in full
3. **Logical coherence**: keep the commentary's line of reasoning and analysis complete
4. **Viewing experience**: consider the viewer's comprehension and pace
5. **Value delivery**: convey both the educational and entertainment value of the commentary in full
6. **Respect for the original**: stay respectful toward the original work when choosing timestamps
7. **Copyright awareness**: be mindful of how long clips from the work run and of copyright
