# Opinion & Commentary Video Timestamp Location Prompt

## Core principles: complete views, clear logic, depth of thought

1.  **Complete views**: timestamps must correspond strictly to the complete expression of a view and its argument.
2.  **Logical coherence**: the time range must fully cover every step of the argument, so the reasoning is complete.
3.  **Natural boundaries**: prefer to place time points at natural boundaries such as a change of view, the end of an argument or an emotional beat.

## Language
Keep the `outline` and `content` text in the same language as the transcript (English for an English video). Do not translate.

---

## Key instructions: how to decide `start_time` and `end_time`

### Deciding `start_time`:
-   Place it at the start time of the **first core statement** of the view.
-   Ignore unrelated transitions, emotional build-up or background before it.
-   **Prefer**: start where the position is clearly stated, so the argument is complete.
-   **Opinion content**: if there is important emotional set-up or a statement of values, move the start earlier accordingly.

### Deciding `end_time` (most important):
-   It **must** be the **end timestamp of the last sentence** covering the core argument of the view.
-   **Complete argument**: make sure the view, the supporting evidence and the judgement are complete, with no gaps in the logic.
-   **Natural ending**: prefer to end at a summarizing sentence, a concluding judgement or a change of view.
-   **Include the emotional payoff**: if the view ends with an important emotional high point or emphasis on values, include it.
-   If the argument continues to the end of the provided SRT chunk, `end_time` should be the end timestamp of the last relevant subtitle.
-   **Wrong approach**: blindly setting `end_time` to the end of the whole chunk. This must be avoided at all costs.

### Duration control for opinion content:
-   **Target length**: each opinion topic segment should be between 3 and 8 minutes
-   **Deep arguments**: in-depth analysis of complex social issues can be extended to 10 minutes
-   **Simple views**: a clear, concise view can be compressed to 2–3 minutes
-   **Controversial topics**: discussion of controversial views can be extended to 12 minutes

### Special handling for opinion content:
-   **Layered arguments**: a view with several layers of argument should keep the whole chain
-   **Comparisons**: pros-and-cons and multi-angle analysis should stay together; avoid splitting them
-   **Judgements**: the position and its judgement should appear together and must not be separated
-   **Emotion**: important emotional high points or emphasis on values should be included in full

---

## Input format
You will receive a JSON object containing:
-   `outline`: a JSON array of **several** opinion topics to process.
-   `srt_text`: the **single** SRT chunk related to this batch of topics, in the format `index\nstart --> end\ntext\n\n`.

## Output format
-   Follow the structure of the input outline exactly and add `start_time` and `end_time` fields to **each** topic object.
-   **Key:** in the output, rename the input `title` field to `outline` (keeping its text exactly), and rename the `subtopics` field to `content`.
-   Output one JSON array containing **all** processed topics.
-   Make sure times use the format `HH:MM:SS,mmm`.

**Strict JSON output requirements:**
1. The output must start with `[` and end with `]`; do not add any explanation, heading or Markdown code-block markers
2. Use standard straight double quotes ", never curly quotes “” or single quotes
3. Make sure all braces and brackets match, objects are separated by commas, and there is no comma after the last object
4. Quotes inside strings must be escaped as \"
5. Do not include comments, extra text or control characters
6. Make sure the JSON is completely valid and can be parsed by a standard JSON parser

## Examples of locating opinion content

### Correct location:
- **Values discussion**: starts at "I think young people today..." and ends at "That's my judgement"
- **Social criticism**: starts at "What this phenomenon shows is..." and ends at "how we should respond"
- **Critical analysis**: starts at "Looking at it from another angle..." and ends at "the overall conclusion"

### Mistakes to avoid:
- Cutting off in the middle of a layered argument
- Separating the position from the argument
- Cutting at a key emotional moment
- Leaving out an important judgement or summary

## Special considerations for opinion content

### 1. Controversial views:
- Include the complete position and argument
- Keep the analysis and reflection from different angles
- Include the basis and criteria for the judgement

### 2. Deep reasoning:
- Keep complex chains of argument complete
- Include the whole multi-dimensional analysis
- Keep the reasoning continuous

### 3. Emotional expression:
- Keep important emotional tone and value positions
- Include the key moment of emotional high point or emphasis
- Keep a balance between emotion and rational analysis

### 4. Social criticism:
- Include the full process of analyzing the problem and suggesting solutions
- Keep the criticism constructive and rational
- Make sure the critical position is clearly expressed

## Notes

1. **Complete views**: make sure each segment contains the complete view and argument
2. **Depth**: important analysis and judgements must be included in full
3. **Logical flow**: keep the chain of argument complete
4. **Emotional balance**: keep rational analysis and emotional expression in harmony
5. **Neutrality**: stay neutral when choosing timestamps and do not judge the views yourself
