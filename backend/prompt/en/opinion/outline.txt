# Opinion & Commentary Video Outline Extraction Prompt

## Input format
You will receive a JSON input:

{
  "text": "{a segment of the video transcript}"
}

- The `text` field is one segment of the video transcript;
- The content has been checked and is broadly coherent;
- Use only the input as your source of information. Do not add outside knowledge, common-sense inferences or logical filler.

## Language
Write every topic name and subtopic in the same language as the transcript (English for an English video). Do not translate.

## Core task: depth of opinion and value for critical thinking
Your task is to **fully cover** every important opinion and argument in the input text while staying **reasonably concise** and avoiding over-fragmentation. The goal is to extract **8–15 core topics**, each corresponding to **3–8 minutes** of video.

## Topic extraction principles

### 1. Value of the opinion first
- **Originality first**: prioritize content with a distinctive perspective, fresh views or deep thinking
- **Controversy**: focus on debatable views that are worth discussing and provoke thought
- **Complete logic**: make sure each view contains a complete argument (claim – evidence – conclusion)

### 2. Depth of thinking
- **Multiple dimensions**: prioritize deep discussion with multi-angle analysis and pros and cons
- **Critical thinking**: focus on content that shows critical, rational analysis
- **Value judgements**: highlight views with a clear value position and criteria for judgement

### 3. Social significance
- **Relevance**: prioritize views related to current social phenomena and news
- **Broad value**: focus on discussion with broad significance that resonates
- **Inspiration**: highlight content that is thought-provoking and broadens thinking

### 4. Complete expression
- **Clear stance**: make sure each topic contains a clear position and attitude
- **Well argued**: keep the argument complete, including facts, logic and emotion
- **Clear conclusion**: make sure the view ends in a clear conclusion or judgement

## Merging and splitting strategy

### Merge (these should become one topic):
- Different levels or angles on the same social phenomenon
- Connected chains of argument
- Different expressions of views within the same value system
- Multi-dimensional analysis of the same issue

### Split (these should become separate topics):
- The position fundamentally changes
- The discussion moves from one field to another
- The argument shifts from rational analysis to emotional expression
- The criteria for judgement clearly change

## Output format

Output strictly in the following format, with no other descriptive text:

### Outline:
1.  **[Top-level topic name]**
    - [Subtopic 1: highlight what is distinctive and thought-provoking about the view]
    - [Subtopic 2: highlight what is distinctive and thought-provoking about the view]
    - …

2.  **[Top-level topic name]**
    - [Subtopic 1]
    - …

Formatting rules (the output is parsed by a program):
- Each topic line starts with its number, a period, and the topic name wrapped in double asterisks: `1.  **Topic name**`
- Each subtopic is on its own line starting with `- `
- Do not use asterisks anywhere else in a topic line, and do not use numbered lines for subtopics

## Output example

### Outline:
1.  **How young people are rebuilding their values**
    - The shift from material goals to meaning and fulfilment
    - Balancing individualism with responsibility to others
    - Paths for blending tradition with modern ideas
    - The conflict between social expectations and personal choice

2.  **A critique of the online filter bubble**
    - How recommendation algorithms shape the way we think
    - The tension between information overload and deep thinking
    - Why we need diverse views and how to get them
    - Why rational judgement matters in an age of information overload

3.  **Education, fairness and social mobility**
    - The root causes of unequal access to education
    - Does "education changes your destiny" still hold today?
    - How broader definitions of success challenge traditional views of education
    - Responses to growing social stratification

## Special considerations for opinion content

### 1. Controversial views:
- Make sure controversial views are expressed completely and fairly
- Include the arguments on different sides
- Keep the original context and emotional tone of the view

### 2. Value judgements:
- Clearly separate statements of fact from judgements of value
- Keep clear statements of value positions
- Include the basis and criteria for the judgement

### 3. Social criticism:
- Keep the criticism constructive and rational
- Include both the analysis of the problem and ideas for solving it
- Avoid pure venting or pointless complaining

## Notes

1. **Complete views**: make sure the extracted topics fully reflect the core position of the original views
2. **Thought-provoking**: prioritize content that invites deep thinking
3. **Social significance**: focus on views with distinctive insight into social phenomena
4. **Clear logic**: make sure the chain of reasoning is complete and the argument clear
5. **Neutrality**: stay neutral while extracting and do not judge the views yourself
