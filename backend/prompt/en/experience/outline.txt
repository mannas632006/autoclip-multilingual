# Experience & How-To Video Outline Extraction Prompt

## Input format
You will receive a JSON input:

{
  "text": "{a segment of the video transcript}"
}

- The `text` field is one segment of the video transcript;
- The content has been checked and is broadly coherent;
- Use only the input as your source of information. Do not add outside knowledge, common-sense inferences or logical filler.

## Language
Write every topic name and subtopic in the same language as the transcript (English for an English video). Do not translate.

## Core task: practical value and actionability together
Your task is to **fully cover** every important experience and technique in the input text while staying **reasonably concise** and avoiding over-fragmentation. The goal is to extract **8–15 core topics**, each corresponding to **3–8 minutes** of video.

## Topic extraction principles

### 1. Practical value first
- **Actionability first**: prioritize experience with clear steps and practical techniques
- **Problem-solving**: focus on experience that solves specific problems and meets real needs
- **No empty talk**: skip purely theoretical discussion and focus on concrete experience that can be copied and practiced

### 2. Complete experience
- **A full method**: make sure each piece of experience contains a complete method (context – method – result – caveats)
- **Rich in detail**: focus on descriptions with specific details and key points
- **Proven results**: highlight experience that includes real results and success stories

### 3. Fit for the audience
- **Reasonable entry level**: prioritize experience that matches the target audience's ability
- **Clear scenarios**: focus on experience with a clear use case and conditions
- **Repeatable**: highlight methods that apply broadly and are easy to copy

### 4. Levels of value
- **Core skills**: prioritize key skills and core methods
- **Advanced techniques**: focus on advanced tips that improve efficiency and results
- **Pitfalls to avoid**: highlight lessons about preventing mistakes and avoiding risks

## Merging and splitting strategy

### Merge (these should become one topic):
- Different aspects or levels of the same skill or method
- Connected steps or stages of a process
- Different solutions or techniques for the same problem
- Different applications in similar situations

### Split (these should become separate topics):
- Experience from different skill areas or use cases
- A clear difference between basic skills and advanced techniques
- Solutions to different types of problems
- Learning the theory separated from applying it in practice

## Output format

Output strictly in the following format, with no other descriptive text:

### Outline:
1.  **[Top-level topic name]**
    - [Subtopic 1: emphasize practicality and actionability]
    - [Subtopic 2: emphasize practicality and actionability]
    - …

2.  **[Top-level topic name]**
    - [Subtopic 1]
    - …

Formatting rules (the output is parsed by a program):
- Each topic line starts with its number, a period, and the topic name wrapped in double asterisks: `1.  **Topic name**`
- Each subtopic is on its own line starting with `- `
- Do not use asterisks anywhere else in a topic line, and do not use numbered lines for subtopics

## Output example

### Outline:
1.  **Effective study methods and techniques in practice**
    - The exact steps and timing of the Pomodoro technique
    - Applying the Feynman technique across different subjects
    - How to build a memory palace and key points for practice
    - Using SMART goals to plan your studying

2.  **Workplace communication skills and real experience**
    - How to prepare for and deliver a point in a meeting
    - Email formatting conventions and things to watch for
    - Communication strategies and conflict handling across teams
    - Reporting to your manager: what to say and when

3.  **Building a personal time-management system**
    - Steps to set up a GTD workflow and choose tools
    - Prioritizing your schedule and allocating time
    - Concrete ways to beat procrastination and manage your mindset
    - Setting and keeping boundaries between work and life

## Special considerations for experience content

### 1. Step-by-step experience:
- Keep the steps complete and in logical order
- Include the key points and caveats
- Give concrete timing and expected results

### 2. Techniques and methods:
- Make clear when and under what conditions the technique applies
- Include specific details and key points for practice
- Give ways to evaluate results and improve

### 3. Problem-solving experience:
- Describe clearly how the problem was identified and analyzed
- Include why a solution was chosen and how it was carried out
- Give ways to verify the result and keep improving

### 4. Pitfalls to avoid:
- Make clear the warning signs of the mistake and how to prevent it
- Include what to do after the mistake happens
- Give strategies for similar situations

## Notes

1. **Practicality check**: make sure the extracted topics have clear practical value and actionability
2. **Completeness**: focus on content with a complete method and specific details
3. **Fit**: prioritize experience that matches the target audience's ability
4. **Repeatability**: make sure the methods apply broadly and can be copied
5. **Levels of value**: distinguish basic skills, advanced techniques and professional experience
